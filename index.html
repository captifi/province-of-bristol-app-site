Everything the Province does, in your pocket

Province of Bristol is a native app for iPhone and Android for subscribing members of Bristol lodges. It turns the Provincial yearbook into a calendar and directory you can filter and search, lets members post degree ceremonies and other events so brethren can visit, carries notices from your own lodge, Provincial news and alerts, and holds the yearbook itself for reading offline.

Who can use it

Access is for subscribing members of lodges in the Province of Bristol. When you first sign in you complete a short setup and an officer of your lodge or the Provincial office approves your request. Nothing beyond the sign-in and setup screens is visible to anyone who is not an approved member.

Not yet a Freemason? The Province welcomes enquiries. See bristolmasons.co.uk for how to join.

What it does

  • Calendar of every meeting in the yearbook, filtered by your lodges, by order or by kind, with installations and Provincial visits marked.
  • Directory of every lodge, chapter and order with meeting rule, officers, past masters and the secretary's contact.
  • Events posted by members: degree ceremonies, Lodges of Instruction, rehearsals and socials, with a push notification to the lodge.
  • Attend a meeting with a properly worded email to the Secretary, ready to send from your own mail app.
  • Lodge notices and summonses from your own officers; Provincial news and alerts from the Provincial office.
  • The yearbook PDF, readable offline, with jump to page from any lodge.
  • My Progress, a private record of your degrees, offices and ranks.
