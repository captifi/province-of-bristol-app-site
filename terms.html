The app and who may use it

The Province of Bristol app is provided by Freemasons of Bristol Limited on behalf of the Provincial Grand Lodge of Bristol for subscribing members of lodges in the Province. By requesting access you confirm that you are such a member and that the details you give are your own. Access is granted, suspended or withdrawn at the discretion of the Province and of the officers of your lodge.

Your account

You sign in with your own email address and a one time code. Do not share codes, and tell the Provincial office if you think someone else has used your account. You are responsible for what is posted from your account.

Using the app properly

  • Post only accurate information about your own lodge's events, and correct or cancel it when things change.
  • Treat the contact details of office holders as for masonic business only. Do not copy them out of the app, share them outside the membership, or use them for marketing.
  • Do not post ritual, anything confidential to a lodge, anything defamatory or unlawful, or anything that would bring the Craft into disrepute.
  • Do not attempt to gain access you have not been granted or to interfere with the service.
  • Officers who use the attendance register, notices and the officer rota do so on behalf of their lodge and in line with its by-laws.

The Province may remove content and suspend or withdraw access for breaches of these terms.

Content you post

You keep ownership of what you post. You give the Province permission to show it to the members entitled to see it, to store it, and to keep a record of it after your account is closed with your name removed. Events, notices and other posts may be edited or removed by the officers of the lodge concerned or by Provincial administrators.

Accuracy

Dates, rooms and details come from the Provincial yearbook and from members, and either may change after publication. Check the summons and the notice board. The Province takes reasonable care but does not guarantee that every entry is correct, and is not responsible for a wasted journey. Please use Report a mistake so we can put errors right quickly.
