Accessibility statement

This statement covers the Province of Bristol app for iPhone and Android and this website. Last reviewed 8 September 2026.

Our commitment

Many of our members are older, and some have reduced eyesight or dexterity. Accessibility was a requirement for the app's first release, not an afterthought. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA in the app and on this site.

What we have done

  • Text respects your phone's text size setting up to 200 percent without clipping; long lists switch to a simpler layout when the text is very large.
  • Colour is never the only signal: every coloured badge or tag also carries its text label, and every foreground and background pair was checked to at least 4.5 to 1 contrast.
  • Every control is at least 44 by 44 points and has a label and, where helpful, a hint for VoiceOver and TalkBack.
  • Dates are announced in full ("Thursday 17 September 2026") even where the screen shows a short tile.
  • Motion respects the Reduce Motion setting; transitions fade instead of sliding.
  • Dark mode is designed rather than inverted, with its own checked colours.
  • The yearbook PDF is the publisher's own file. Its text can be searched from within the app, and every lodge can be reached by name rather than by finding a page.

Known limitations

  • The yearbook PDF itself is a scanned and typeset document produced outside the app; its pages are images with a text layer and are not fully navigable by screen reader. Use the app's own directory and calendar for the same information.
  • Articles in the News section come from other organisations' websites and open in a browser view; their accessibility is theirs.

Tell us

If anything in the app or on this site gets in your way, email prov.sec@provinceofbristol.org.uk or use Suggestions in the app under Settings, Help. We would rather hear about it than have you struggle.
