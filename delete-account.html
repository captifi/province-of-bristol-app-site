Delete your account

You can delete your account yourself in the app at any time. You do not need to ask anyone.

In the app

  1. Open the app and tap the gear on the Home screen to open Settings.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

You are signed out straight away and the deletion completes within 30 days.

By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email prov.sec@provinceofbristol.org.uk from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Province of Bristol app account". We confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign in details.
  • Your profile: name, rank, Mother Lodge, style of address, sign off.
  • Your lodge memberships and any Provincial roles.
  • Notification settings and device push tokens.
  • Your attendance marks, app opens, calendar additions and read markers.
  • Your My Progress record.
  • Offers you made to fill an officer's chair, and suggestions you sent.

What is kept

  • Events, lodge notices and officer requests you created remain for the lodge's record, with your name removed and no link back to you.
  • Attendance register entries made about you by a lodge officer remain in that lodge's register as a record of the meeting; ask the lodge Secretary if you want them removed.
  • Sign in logs are kept for 90 days for security and then removed.

Deleting the app is not the same

Removing the app from your phone does not delete your account. Use one of the two routes above.

Coming back

You can request access again at any time by signing in with your email address and completing setup. Approval works as it did the first time.
