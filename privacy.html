Privacy policy

Effective from 1 October 2026. Applies to the Province of Bristol app for iPhone and Android and to this website.

Who we are

The Province of Bristol app is published by Freemasons of Bristol Limited (the publisher of the Provincial yearbook) on behalf of the Provincial Grand Lodge of Bristol. Freemasons of Bristol Limited is the data controller. Our address is Freemasons' Hall, 31 Park Street, Bristol BS1 5NH. For anything about your personal data, email prov.sec@provinceofbristol.org.uk or write to the Provincial Secretary at that address.

Who the app is for

The app is for subscribing members of lodges in the Province of Bristol. It is not intended for anyone under 18. Access is granted by an officer of your lodge or by the Provincial office after you request it in the app.

Information taken from the yearbook

The app contains information from the Provincial yearbook that members already receive in print: lodge names and meeting dates, the names of officers and past masters as printed, and the role email addresses and telephone numbers of lodge secretaries, scribes and Provincial officers. We publish this in the app on the basis of legitimate interests: it exists so that members can contact office holders. We do not import anyone's home address, and In Memoriam pages are not included. Any office holder may ask the Provincial Secretary to hide their contact details or name in the app.

Who can see what

  • Nothing in the directory, calendar, events, notices or news is visible until your access has been approved.
  • Lodge notices and attendance registers are visible only to members and officers of that lodge.
  • Your My Progress record is visible only to you. Provincial administrators cannot read it.
  • If you offer to fill an officer's chair at another lodge, your name, Mother Lodge, rank and email address are shared with that lodge's officers only if they accept your offer, and you are told this before you offer.
  • Suggestions you send to the Provincial office are readable by Provincial administrators.

Who processes data for us

We use these suppliers, each acting on our instructions:

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign in service and file storage, in its London (United Kingdom) region.
  • Expo delivers updates to the app's code and relays push notifications to Apple and Google. Notifications never contain contact details.
  • Sentry receives crash reports with email addresses removed.
  • Apple and Google deliver push notifications to your device under their own terms.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires.

Content from other websites

The News section shows articles gathered automatically from the public websites of the Province of Bristol, the United Grand Lodge of England, neighbouring Provinces and, where enabled, press coverage. Each article names its source and links to the original, which opens in a browser view inside the app. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything attached to it: until you delete your account, then removed within 30 days.
  • Events, notices and requests you created: kept for the lodge's record with your name removed once your account is deleted.
  • Sign in logs: 90 days.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you may ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent at any time. Most of these you can do yourself in the app: edit your profile in Settings, turn notifications off, and delete your account under Settings, Account. For anything else email prov.sec@provinceofbristol.org.uk. We respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Deleting your account

Open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. Your profile, memberships, notification settings, push tokens, attendance marks and My Progress record are removed within 30 days. See Delete your account for the full description.

Security

Sign in uses a one time code sent to your email; there are no passwords to steal. Every request to the database is checked against your approval status and lodge memberships on the server. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Changes

When this policy changes we update the date at the top and tell members in the app. Earlier versions are available from the Provincial Secretary on request.
