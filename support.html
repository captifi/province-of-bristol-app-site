Support

The quickest route for most questions is your lodge Secretary. For anything about the app itself, contact the Provincial office.

Email prov.sec@provinceofbristol.org.uk.

Inside the app, Settings then Help has the same details, a Suggestions form, and Report a mistake on every lodge and every date.

Signing in

My code has not arrived

Codes come from the Province by email within a minute. Check your junk folder, and check the address you typed. Tap Resend code to get a fresh one; each code works once and expires after an hour. If nothing arrives, your email provider may be blocking the message: email the Provincial office from the same address and we will look into it.

I entered the code and nothing happened

Make sure the app can reach the internet, then try again. If the code has expired, request a new one.

Getting approved

After setup your request goes to the officers of the lodges you named and to the Provincial office. Most requests are decided within two days. Until then you can see only the setup and pending screens. If it has been longer than a week, email the office with your name and lodge.

If your address is on the Province's list of member emails, approval is immediate.

Notifications

Notifications are off until you turn them on in Settings, Notifications. Each category has a switch: events at your lodges, Provincial news, alerts, lodge notices, officers needed, reminders and the weekly digest. If you turned them on and see nothing, check that notifications are allowed for the app in your phone's own Settings.

Something in the yearbook is wrong

Every lodge page and every calendar entry has Report a mistake, which opens an email to the office with the record reference already filled in. Corrections are pushed to the app without waiting for a new version.

My lodge is missing or I am in the wrong lodges

Open Settings, My Lodges to add or remove lodges. Your Mother Lodge is set on the Profile screen and must be a Craft lodge. Officer status for a lodge is set by the Provincial office.

The app says I need to update

Occasionally a change needs a new version from the App Store or Google Play. The app tells you when, with a link. Smaller updates install themselves the next time you open the app.

Deleting your account

Settings, Account, Delete account. See Delete your account for what is removed.

Accessibility

The app supports large text, VoiceOver and TalkBack. See the accessibility statement, and tell us if anything gets in your way.
